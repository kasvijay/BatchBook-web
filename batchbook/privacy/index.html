This Privacy Policy describes how ZunicLabs ("we", "us", "our", "the Company") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the BatchBook mobile application and any related services (collectively, "the App"). BatchBook is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store).

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time; the "Effective date" above will reflect the most recent revision.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all users of the App, including:

  • Teachers / Tutors who use the BatchBook Teacher app to manage students, batches, attendance, fees, and test reports.
  • Parents / Guardians who use the BatchBook Parent app to view their child's attendance and fee information.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain non-personally-identifiable information through our analytics services:

  • Usage events: Actions you take in the App (e.g., "student created", "attendance marked", "payment recorded"). These events contain aggregate or categorical metadata (e.g., number of students in a batch, fee type selected, payment mode used) but never contain personal data such as names, phone numbers, or addresses.
  • App lifecycle events: When the App is opened, backgrounded, or sessions begin.
  • Screen views: Which screens you navigate to within the App.
  • Device and environment metadata: Operating system, app version, and whether the app is running in a debug or release environment.

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone input, browsing history, email address, passwords, biometric data, financial account numbers, credit card details, device advertising identifiers, or any government-issued identification.

3.3 Information from Third-Party Platforms

If you download the App from Google Play or the Apple App Store, those platforms may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

4. How Your Data is Stored

4.1 Local Storage (On-Device)

BatchBook is designed as an offline-first application. All student, batch, attendance, fee, payment, and test report data you enter is stored locally on your device using the platform's native database (Room on Android, SwiftData on iOS). This data never leaves your device except in the specific circumstances described below.

4.2 iCloud Sync (iOS Only)

On iOS, the App uses Apple's CloudKit framework to automatically sync your data to your personal iCloud account. This provides backup and multi-device access. This sync is governed by your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data. You can manage iCloud storage and sync through your device's Settings.

4.3 No Company-Operated Servers

As of the effective date of this policy, we do not operate backend servers that store your data. There is no user account, login, or cloud database managed by ZunicLabs. If we introduce cloud sync or backend services in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly, and you will be notified before any data is transmitted to our servers.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • Core functionality: To provide the tuition management features of the App (managing students, marking attendance, tracking fees, recording payments, generating test reports).
  • Local notifications: To send you on-device reminders about pending attendance and upcoming/overdue fee due dates, based on your configured reminder settings.
  • Analytics and improvement: To understand how the App is used in aggregate, identify bugs, and improve features. Analytics data is non-personally-identifiable.
  • Crash reporting: To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and errors (when Firebase Crashlytics is enabled).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, selling, or renting to third parties.

The App allows you to send pre-composed messages through WhatsApp (e.g., fee reminders, payment receipts, attendance summaries). 6.3 Apple iCloud (iOS Only)

As described in Section 4.2, your data may be synced to your iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

6.4 System Share Sheet

The App offers a system share feature (e.g., "Share App"). When you use this feature, data is shared through your device's native sharing mechanism to the app or contact you choose. We have no control over the destination.

6.5 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to any third party for any purpose, including marketing, advertising, or data brokering.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: Your data remains on your device for as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data. On iOS, data synced to iCloud will persist in your iCloud account until you manually delete it.
  • Analytics data: Usage events sent to PostHog and Firebase are retained according to those services' data retention policies. We do not control their retention periods, but this data is non-personally-identifiable.
  • No server-side retention: Since we do not operate servers that store your personal data, there is no server-side data to retain or delete.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • On-device encryption: Data stored on your device benefits from your device's built-in hardware encryption (when a passcode/biometric lock is enabled).
  • Secure transmission: All analytics data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Minimal data collection: We follow a data minimization approach — analytics events contain only categorical and aggregate metadata, never personal data.
  • No PII in analytics: Our analytics implementation is designed to never transmit personally identifiable information (names, phone numbers, addresses) to any third-party service.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including setting a passcode and keeping your operating system updated.

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • View, edit, or delete any student, batch, attendance, fee, payment, or test report record directly within the App.
